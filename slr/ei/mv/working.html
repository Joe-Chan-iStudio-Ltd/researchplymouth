Executive Summary: Research Architecture

1. The Problem

Deconstructing the "intent–action gap" where high career intentions fail to materialise into venture creation due to high HK reservation wages.

2. The Framework

The Passion Ecosystem Model (PEM): Integrating affective engines (Passion) into cognitive models to override economic trade-offs.

3. Methodology

Explanatory sequential mixed-methods. PLS-SEM ($n \ge 135$) followed by purposive qualitative interviews via HKMU-DAAO.

4. Contribution

Theoretical path validation + a tangible Open-Science Web Repository for regional entrepreneurship researchers.

1. Introduction & Research Positionality

The Scholar-Practitioner Perspective

  • Professional Role: Programmer at DSD, HKSAR Government.
  • Academic Core: Computing (OUHK) & MBA (HKMU).
  • Motivation: Bridging technical infrastructure with entrepreneurial ecosystems.

The HK-GBA Context

Hong Kong represents a unique institutional paradox: world-class financial capitalization ($GSEI$ 35th globally) vs. micro-foundational creative bottlenecks.
