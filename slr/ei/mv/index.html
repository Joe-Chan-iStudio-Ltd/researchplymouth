1. Introduction: The HK-GBA Context

The Paradox of Success

  • Macro Growth: HK ranked 35th globally; 26.6% annual surge in ecosystem score (StartupBlink, 2026).
  • The Gap: Massive capital infusions fail to naturally activate organic student venture volume.
  • Opportunity Costs: Lucrative corporate wages inflate reservation wages, deterring youth from high-uncertainty paths.
