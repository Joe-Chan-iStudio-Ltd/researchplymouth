1.1 Introduction: Context & The Economic Paradox

Theoretical Foundations

  • Schumpeterian Creation (“CRT”): Mandates radical creative destruction to shatter market equilibria and catalyze deep structural transformations.
  • Kirznerian Discovery (“DIS”): Focuses on alertness and imitative arbitrage; risks rapid market saturation if over-replicated within an ecosystem.

Scholar-Practitioner Positionality

Programmer analytics: Bridging macro infrastructure with micro execution.

The HK-GBA Institutional Matrix

  • 2026 Macro Growth: GSEI ranks HK 35th globally with a 26.6% annual surge in ecosystem score, backing over 4,600 active startups.
  • The Reservation Wage Bottleneck: Lucrative corporate pathways within HK’s hyper-free economy artificially inflate alternative reservation wages for university graduates.
  • Socio-Economic Friction: Exorbitant local living costs and risk-averse cultures drive premium talent away from high-uncertainty trajectories.

1.2 Problem Statement & Research Design

The Problem Statement

The "Intent–Action Gap": Regional research relies on a cold, purely cognitive hyper-focus, repeatedly verifying static intentions via traditional TPB models while omitting emotional affect.

  • Affective Engine: Entrepreneurial Passion (“EP”) is required to override high reservation wages, yet remains unexamined in HK student contexts.
  • Institutional Lacuna: Near-zero representation of INS and Knowledge Spillover Theory in local student entrepreneurship data.

Core Objective

Deconstruct socio-cognitive mechanisms translating passive intentions into market entry in the HK-GBA corridor.

Phase 1: Quantitative Engine

PLS-SEM Analysis (n ≥ 135)
RQ1-3: Mapping how traits and ecosystem support (PES) trigger multidimensional EP and moderate behavioral intent.

Phase 2: Qualitative Nuance

Thematic Analysis (n = 10~20)
RQ4: Uncovering localized risk thresholds and cultural barriers via secure HKMU-DAAO channels.

2.1 Literature Review: Methodology & Granularity Gap

SLR Processing & Extraction

  • Multi-Database Sweep: Systematic extraction across EBSCO, Scopus, and Web of Science using CIMO structural mapping (n=285).
  • Quality-First Filtering: Global baseline restricted to elite AJG 4*-ranked theoretical journals (e.g., ET&P, JBV) to capture core paradigms.
  • Regional Counter-Bias: Core sweep supplemented by targeted HK-specific grey literature to isolate highly localized economic and cultural parameters.

The Localized Empirical Vacuum

  • Methodological Granularity Gap: Global studies utilize robust multi-source or longitudinal datasets; HK data relies almost exclusively on static surveys.
  • Paradigm Representation (n=0): Foundational growth frameworks—Human Capital (“HCT”), Knowledge Spillover (“KST”), Upper Echelons (“UET”), and Schumpeterian Creation theory—possess near-zero empirical execution in local cohorts.

2.2 Cognitive Intent vs. Affective Drive

Cognitive Paradigms & Distortions

  • Aristotelian Epistemic Split: Bridging positivist quantitative path boundaries (Episteme) with constructivist qualitative narrative wisdom (Phronesis).
  • Cross-Cultural TPB Violations: Collective Chinese socio-cultural expectations heavily moderate and constrain the baseline link between individual ESE and behavioral intention.

Behavioral Economics & Affect

  • The Alternative Reservation Wage: Lucrative corporate pathways generate severe opportunity costs, driving rational youth away from high-uncertainty trajectories.
  • The Affective Insulation: Overriding this utility boundary requires cognitive distortions (Optimism bias, inverted Loss Aversion) sustained directly by the multi-dimensional Entrepreneurial Passion (“EP”) engine.

2.3 Paradox: Regional Milieu vs. Stanford

The Stanford Archetype

  • Systemic De-risking: Institutional structures minimize the socio-economic penalties and cultural stigmas of venture failure.
  • Agglomeration Mechanics: Deep institutionalized knowledge networks, specialized TTO infrastructure, and high density of local VC capital asset sourcing.

The Hong Kong Contextual Friction

  • Conservative Kirznerian Legacy: Ecosystem historically optimized for short-term financial flexibility, property speculation, and imitative arbitrage.
  • Structural Inhibitors: High real estate friction, family face-saving pressures, and acute deficits in long-term seed resources restrain Schumpeterian technical creation.

2.4 Institutional Catalysts & Micro-Support

Cross-Boundary GBA Infrastructure Support (“GIS”)

Replicating massive Western agglomeration effects by merging HK's top-tier foundational research capacity with the dense manufacturing, industrial scaling, and capitalization networks of Mainland China. Bypasses localized constraints via multi-networking ecosystem hybridization.

Non-Linear Corridor Volatility

EO-performance links in emerging markets are strictly curvilinear. Stabilizing this volatility requires internal Learning Orientations (“LO”) alongside institutional trust networks.

ESO Multi-Stakeholder Alignment

Modeling Perceived Ecosystem Support (“PES”) through five archetypes (Incubators, parks, TTOs). Maximizes output by balancing formal structural tracking with founder operational autonomy.

3.1 Sequential Mixed-Methods Architecture

Phase 1: Quantitative Core

Causal-Predictive Modeling (Deductive)

  • Engine: PLS-SEM path analysis in SmartPLS 4 to validate PEM structural paths.
  • Sample: n ≥ 135 valid responses across local tertiary institutions.
  • Goal: Compute path coefficients between traits, passion, and behavioral intent.

Phase 2: Qualitative Nuance

Explanatory Contextualization (Inductive)

  • Engine: Thematic analysis via NVivo to decode statistical boundaries.
  • Sample: 10–20 in-depth semi-structured interviews with nascent student founders.
  • Access: Purposive sampling brokered via strategic HKMU-DAAO incubation lines.

Post-Positivist Paradigm Realignment

The structural architecture consciously blends "cold" cognitive filters with "hot" affective drives to accurately map human action. This integrated perspective allows the design to quantitatively validate causal mechanisms while qualitatively decoding hidden behavioral variances within Hong Kong's distinct socio-economic corridor.

3.2 Data Management & Governance

Data Collection Capture

Primary datasets are captured securely via ISO 27001 certified platforms (Jisc Online Surveys) and institutional Zoom channels. Audio-visual qualitative data tracking files are systematically anonymized during the transcription workflow phase.

Secure Storage Lifecycle

Centralized entirely on the University of Plymouth’s institutional Microsoft OneDrive architecture using 256-bit AES encryption profiles. Embedded SharePoint versioning engines are activated to maintain continuous Point in Time Restore (“PITR”).

Long-Term Preservation & Compliance Infrastructure

PEARL Repository Retention

Anonymized core research datasets are preserved for a minimum lifecycle of 5 years within the PEARL repository, establishing long-term data discoverability via static Digital Object Identifiers (“DOI”).

Governance & Access Control

  • Local encryption via BitLocker/FileVault with MFA.
  • Absolute redaction of identifying names.
  • GDPR, UK Data Protection Act 2018, and CC BY-NC.
